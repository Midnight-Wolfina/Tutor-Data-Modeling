R5 - Hypotheses as If-Then with Testable Predictions 

Need 2 hypotheses (must be if-then and not easily guessed)

Hidden Variable: How much are they struggling?
There could be many reasons why they are struggling:
1. They could be a new student or there is a language issue.
2. Personal and Health reasons
3. They have weak skills in a certain subject 


Hypothesis 1: If tutoring is actually working, then students with the same prior GPA would be less likely to fail.

Hypothesis 2: If the tutored students are causing the failure, then they have lower prior GPA's compared to untutored students.

Test:
1. We could group the students based on what their prior GPA was.
2. Compare the failure rate between tutored and non-tutored students per group. 
3. Decide how failing is measured and what counts as helping.
